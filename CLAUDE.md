# Musical Bio (musical.bio)

Lionel's link-in-bio app. `musical.bio/lionelyu` is the landing page linked from his social accounts, so it must
stay up. `main` deploys production on Vercel. A/B testing on the bio page: `docs/ab-testing.md`.

## The commander

Lionel's requests now usually come through his commander session (`~/Documents/Lionel Command Center`). A brief
arrives as one line ("Brief #N … cmdr inbox KEY"); the brief itself, with his words verbatim, is in the hub:
`cmdr inbox KEY`. Report back with `cmdr report`, keep this project's card on his board current with
`cmdr status`, and put decisions for him on the board with `cmdr ask`, always with a default. Rules and words:
`~/Documents/Lionel Command Center/protocol/COMMANDER.md`. This project's key: `bio`.
